# Identidad del planificador

`planificador-premium.png` es el original generado con la herramienta integrada
de imagenes y aprobado por el usuario. No se utilizaron claves de IA del aplicativo.
El logo combina una P y un libro en grafito, marfil, champagne y verde esmeralda.

## Derivados

Desde la raiz: `java scripts/GenerarIconos.java`.

Genera PNG opacos `src/main/resources/static/images/pwa/*-v2.png` para el navegador,
la interfaz, Android y iPhone. Solo redimensiona la imagen aprobada; la variante
adaptativa agrega margen para conservar el monograma dentro de las mascaras.
Los archivos de la primera version se conservan para clientes que todavia no han
actualizado su worker. El original no se descarga desde las pantallas del aplicativo.

## Prompt Original

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single exceptionally refined premium mobile app icon
for Planificador Academico, a university academic planning app. Art direction: a
bespoke luxury technology brand, sophisticated and contemporary, not a playful
generic productivity icon. Full-bleed square background in very dark neutral
graphite black with an extremely subtle fine satin surface, no outer frame and no
pre-rounded app tile. One large central custom sculptural P monogram ingeniously
formed from a folded book cover: smooth luminous porcelain-ivory main ribbon with
a subtle champagne brushed-metal inner fold and a restrained deep emerald inset.
Only two or three precise page edges at the foot subtly communicate a book.
Strong elegant geometric silhouette with generous negative space, meticulous
proportions and beautiful optical balance, instantly identifiable even at 48
pixels. Premium physical product rendering with fine bevels and controlled soft
studio highlights, understated tactile depth, not inflated plastic, not cartoon,
not gaudy gold or chrome. Keep every meaningful part of the mark in the central
65 percent of the square for Android maskable and iPhone crop safety. The P
monogram is the entire logo. No additional text, words, captions, numbers,
graduation cap, checkmark, stars, ornaments, floating particles, halo, bokeh,
surrounding objects, watermark, device mockup, comparison sheet or decorative
border. A polished final standalone app icon, visibly more elegant and
expensive-looking than standard app artwork.
